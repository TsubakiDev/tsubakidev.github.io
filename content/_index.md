## What I’m exploring

### Neuromorphic intelligence

Spiking neural networks, memory-based models, and architectures that can learn with less data, energy, and latency.

### Systems that stay out of the way

Reliable software for real-time workloads, from low-level networking and concurrency to efficient inference pipelines.

### Research made tangible

I like turning ambitious ideas into small, testable systems — preferably in `Rust`, with clear interfaces and measurable behavior.
